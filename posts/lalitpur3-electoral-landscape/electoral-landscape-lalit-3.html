Diagnostic Analysis of Past Election Results

In this section we will analyse the electoral landscape of the Lalitpur - 3 based on past two election at federal, provincial and local level. It attempts diagnoses past election outcomes to understand how votes were distributed, transferred, and consolidated across parties and candidates. It establishes the empirical foundation upon which the strategic analysis in later sections is built.

FPTP - Federal Election



Observation : Making sense of electoral win of Ms Toshima Karki

  • Bibeksheel Sajha managed to get 12.9% vote in the election of 2074. This in intself is an indication of demand for newer face in the constituency !

  • NC, which was a significant political force, opted to support CPN-Moaist candidate in the election.

Beside other obvious and immediate political circumstances, above two reasons provided an impetus to RSP resulting a big margin of victory, which otherwise, could have been a tight race.

Electoral Opportunity & Strategic Landscape - Direction for the way ahead !

This section provides a strategic reading of the constituency’s electoral landscape, focusing on where political competition exists and where it does not. Instead of treating the constituency as a single homogeneous unit, the analysis breaks it down ward-by-ward to assess fragmentation, competitiveness, and voter volatility.

The indicators presented here—ENP, MoV, ILV, and the Composite Index—are designed to answer three practical campaign questions:

  • Where are voters most open to alternatives?
  • Where are margins narrow enough to realistically flip?
  • Where does volatility suggest weak party consolidation?

The output is a clear prioritisation of wards, enabling evidence-based campaign planning rather than intuition-driven targeting.

ENP - Effective Number of Parties

Concept

ENP measures party fragmentation — how many parties are effectively competing in a constituency, weighted by their vote share.

Low ENP → few dominant parties

High ENP → many parties with similar vote shares

Strategic Value

  1. Comparative Evaluation of general party fragmentation among other constituency or at ward level.

  2. Prepare ward-level strategy

    • Wards with high ENP suggests marginal or small disruption creates a greater impact for new entrants – Non polarised voter signals opportunity for new ideas and ppl.

    • Ward with low ENP : Strategise based on people’s percieved grievances against establashed dominant parties.

  3. Identify wards or segments of wards (such as provincial constituency) with high ENP that gives a good entry point for new entrants

  4. Uniform political campaign strategy for groups of wards (as categories in Table 4)

Indicator II : Margin of Victory (MoV)

Concept

MoV reflects competitiveness. It is calcuated as a differece (in percentage point of vote share) between the winner and runner-up (rank 1 and 2).

Smaller MoV -> Higher chances of contestability or voters are tightly divided

Larger MoV -> Strong dominance by a single party or voters significantly more inclined towards a dominant party.

Strategic Value

  1. The federal MoV, at prime facie, a measure how tightly the constituency is contested with. Lower MoV value is a favourable condition, at large, for the new entrants

  2. Identify segments of wards or invidiual wards with similar traits to formulate similar plans :

    1. For example in wards with Lower MoV: Higher chances of contestability
    2. In case of ward with higher MoV: suggests strong dominance by a single party and hence the startegy could be combination of anti-incumbency agendas along with other relevant measures based on the dominant party.

MoV under microscope - Intra-Local Volatality (ILV)

Concept

ILV measures how much voter preference fluctuates within a ward across elections.

Higher ILV → Suggests higher fluctuation Lower ILV → Suggest lower flucturation

Strategic Value

  1. ILV is a measure of spread of MoV values within each ward. Generally, higher ILV (higher flactuation) provides strategic advantage for new political options as voters have not consolidated to any political force.

  2. ILV along with MoV (for each ward) can be used to identify individual wards with strategic values and formulate campaigns plans accordingly.

Winnability Index (Composite Index of ILV and MoV)


The winnability Index is a composite index of ILV and MoV that are combined to provide a single actionable indicator.
